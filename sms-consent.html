SMS Consent

Last updated: March 1, 2026

Striking Music offers optional text message (SMS) notifications to keep families informed about lesson scheduling, monthly statements, and important updates. Use the form below to opt in to receive text messages from us.

What You'll Receive

  • Monthly lesson statements and payment confirmations
  • Lesson scheduling changes, reschedules, and confirmations
  • Important reminders and updates related to your student's lessons

Message frequency is typically 1–4 messages per month. This is a transactional messaging program — we do not send marketing or promotional messages. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Opt In to SMS Notifications

Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of enrollment. Your phone number and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third party. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. Contact Ethan@Striking-Music.com with any questions.
